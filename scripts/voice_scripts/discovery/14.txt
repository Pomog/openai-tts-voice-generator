I think I’ve been doing Project Discovery completely wrong.
I was carefully tracing every cluster, trying to get the best accuracy possible.
But on the empty samples — the ones without density lines along the axes — there’s a much faster way.
David Garth and DenVer gave me this trick: click three times, then click outside the box. It closes the polygon automatically, and you can submit almost instantly.
No careful tracing. Just four clicks, submit, and move on.
You can repeat it ridiculously fast - apparently, a little too fast.
Four clicks. Submit. Next sample. That’s it.
